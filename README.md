## Android volunteers wanted

We aim to recruit **15–20 real volunteer testers** for honest usage and feedback once the official closed test opens. This is our recruitment goal, not Google's minimum requirement. Participation is free; no payment is requested.

[Register interest in Issue #1](https://github.com/ichat999-2026/iChat999-Downloads/issues/1). You may share your Android version and optionally your device model. **Signing up here is interest registration only—not live beta access, Play enrollment or an installation invitation.** It does not start the testing period.

Do not post passwords, verification codes, phone numbers, personal chats, payment details or account recovery information. No reciprocal testing of other apps, ratings or positive reviews are required or promised.

For personal Play developer accounts covered by [Google's testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en), at least **12 testers must remain opted in continuously for 14 days** before the developer can apply for production access. Google also assesses testing engagement and feedback; meeting the minimum is not automatic release approval.
