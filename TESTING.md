# Android volunteer tester guide

Updated **October 10, 2026**. Android version 1.0.0, versionCode 3 is a **Google Play closed-test draft**, with 0 testers and App setup 82%. There is no verified Play opt-in link, Google Group invitation or public APK. This is not a live beta.

## Register interest, then wait for an invitation

1. Comment in [Issue #1](https://github.com/ichat999-2026/iChat999-Downloads/issues/1) if you would like to volunteer. Your Android version and optional device model are enough; do not post account or contact details.
2. Wait for verified enrollment and installation instructions through the [official availability page](https://ichat999.com/#download) or this repository. Interest registration is not Play enrollment and does not start the testing period.
3. When the official test is available, follow its actual Play opt-in instructions before installing. A repository comment or Google Group membership alone is not enrollment. Use your own eligible ordinary account; never share your login credentials.

Our goal is **15–20 real volunteers**, not an official minimum. Participation is free and voluntary. We do not require or promise reciprocal testing of other apps, ratings or positive reviews.

For accounts covered by [Google's testing requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en), the developer needs at least **12 testers opted in continuously for 14 days** before applying for production access. We need honest use and feedback, not just account counts. Approval is not automatic. You can leave voluntarily; opting out interrupts the continuous period.

## Useful, safe feedback

Once invited, try the features available in the test build using non-sensitive test content. Report the app version, Android version, steps, expected result and actual result in the issue or through [support](https://ichat999.com/support).

Never send passwords, verification codes, phone numbers, personal chats, payment details or recovery information. Redact screenshots. Do not pay for access, install unofficial packages or disable Play Protect. Check the app's [eligibility and safety rules](https://ichat999.com/terms) before participating.
