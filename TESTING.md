# Help test iChat999

Updated October 17, 2026. Participation is free and voluntary. We welcome honest feedback; no ratings, positive reviews or reciprocal testing are required or promised.

## Get started

1. Check the [available builds and limitations](INSTALL.md).
2. Use non-sensitive sample content to try the functions available in your build. Windows is an offline UI preview, not a connected messaging client. Android website top-ups are unavailable.
3. Report a problem in [Issues](https://github.com/ichat999-2026/iChat999-Downloads/issues) or through [support](https://ichat999.com/support).

For a useful report, include your app version, platform/OS version, steps to reproduce, expected and actual results, and a redacted screenshot or error if helpful.

Never post passwords, verification codes, phone numbers, private conversations, payment details or account recovery information. Keep private reports out of public issues. Do not disable device security protections.

## Future Google Play testing

You can express volunteer interest through [Issues](https://github.com/ichat999-2026/iChat999-Downloads/issues). Interest registration and website APK use are **not** Google Play closed-test enrollment and do not start any required testing period. Verified Play opt-in instructions will be published when available.

Read the [eligibility and safety rules](https://ichat999.com/terms) before participating.
